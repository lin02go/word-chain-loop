HOW TO PLAY

  1. 输入至少 3 个字母的英文词。
  2. 新词要接住上一个词的最后两个字母。
  3. 不能重复单词，也不能换个词形重复使用。
  4. 让最后一个词的末两位回到起始词的前两位。
  5. 用词越少越好；使用提示后，本局记为练习。
  1. Play an English word with at least 3 letters.
  2. Begin with the previous word’s final two letters.
  3. Do not repeat a word or its inflections.
  4. Return to the first word’s opening pair.
  5. Fewer words is better; hints make it practice-only.

人名、缩写、数字与标点不计入词库。Names, acronyms, digits and punctuation are excluded.
